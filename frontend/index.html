The distinction

OpenAPI and DataMaster have different jobs.

OpenAPI describes the contract of one HTTP API. DataMaster consumes contracts and maintains ecosystem state: ownership, canonical mappings, dependents, impact analysis, adapters, provenance, and approval history.

OpenAPI describes an API. DataMaster manages the dependency and integration lifecycle across many APIs.
